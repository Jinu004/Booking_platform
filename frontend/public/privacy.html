01

Who We Are

Receptionai (trading as ReceptionAI, registered under Udyam, Kerala, India) operates receptionai.in — an AI-powered WhatsApp appointment booking and clinic reception platform for private clinics in India.

This Privacy Policy explains how ReceptionAI collects, uses, stores, and protects information received through our platform, including through WhatsApp conversations facilitated on behalf of our clinic partners.

By interacting with a clinic's WhatsApp number powered by ReceptionAI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information directly from patients via WhatsApp conversations, and indirectly through clinic partners who use our platform.

Data Type Source Purpose
WhatsApp phone number Meta / WhatsApp Identify and contact you for bookings
Name Provided by you during conversation Personalise responses and booking records
Conversation messages WhatsApp chat with the clinic Process AI responses; booking assistance
Appointment details Booking conversation / clinic Schedule, confirm, and remind appointments
Visit history Stored after each appointment Follow-ups; service continuity

We do not collect payment card details, government IDs, precise location, or biometric data.

03

How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used solely to deliver the appointment booking service on behalf of the clinic you contacted. Specific uses include:

  • Responding to your booking requests and queries through WhatsApp
  • Creating and managing appointment records for the clinic
  • Sending appointment confirmation and reminder messages
  • Enabling the clinic to view and manage their patient appointments
  • Improving service reliability and identifying technical issues

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with any party not listed in Section 5.

04

WhatsApp Messaging

Our platform relies on WhatsApp to facilitate bookings:

  • We use the Meta WhatsApp Business API to securely send and receive messages on behalf of clinics.
  • Messages from ReceptionAI are only sent in response to patient-initiated conversations or as appointment reminders the patient consented to by booking.
  • Patients can stop receiving messages at any time by not initiating further conversations or by requesting opt-out through the clinic.
05

Third-Party Services

To deliver our service, your data is processed by the following third parties:

Service Provider What is shared Purpose
WhatsApp Cloud API Meta Platforms, Inc. Messages, phone number Message delivery infrastructure
Gemini AI Google LLC Conversation message content AI-generated booking responses

Note on AI processing: Your message content is sent to Google Gemini to generate automated responses. Google processes this data under its own data processing agreements. Message content is not stored by Google for model training when accessed via the API.

We do not share your data with any other third party.

06

Data Storage & Security

All data is stored on servers located in Mumbai, India (Google Cloud Platform). We implement the following security measures:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Database access restricted to backend services only — no public exposure
  • Tenant isolation — each clinic's data is logically separated and inaccessible to other clinics
  • Rate limiting on all API endpoints to prevent abuse
  • Regular automated backups

In the event of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify the affected clinic partner within 72 hours of becoming aware of the incident.

07

Data Retention

We retain your data for 24 months from your last interaction with any clinic using the ReceptionAI platform. After this period, your data is permanently deleted from our systems.

A clinic may request earlier deletion of their patient records at any time by contacting us at support@receptionai.in. Individual patients may also request deletion directly (see Section 9).

08

Clinic Partners

ReceptionAI provides its platform to clinics as a data processor. The clinic you interacted with is the data controller for your appointment and medical records — they determine the purpose and means of processing your clinical data.

Clinics using our platform are responsible for:

  • Obtaining patient consent for data collection as required under applicable law
  • Maintaining their own patient privacy policies
  • Complying with applicable health data regulations

ReceptionAI processes data on behalf of clinics solely to provide the agreed booking and communication service.

09

Your Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to grievance redressal — raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (see Section 12)

To exercise any of these rights, email support@receptionai.in with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

10

Children's Privacy

Our platform is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. Appointments for minors may be booked by a parent or guardian, in which case the adult's consent covers the booking.

If you believe a minor's data has been collected without appropriate consent, please contact us at support@receptionai.in and we will promptly delete it.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated to clinic partners via email.

Continued use of any clinic's WhatsApp number powered by ReceptionAI after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
