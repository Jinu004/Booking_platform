Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ReceptionAI. We are a conversational booking platform that helps clinics manage appointments seamlessly via WhatsApp.

This Privacy Policy explains how ReceptionAI (operated at receptionai.in) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide our appointment booking services:

  • Personal Information: Name and phone number (provided by the patient during the booking process).
  • Appointment Details: Specific details related to your booking, such as the selected doctor, date, and token number.
  • Communications: WhatsApp messages sent to and from clinic numbers managed by ReceptionAI.

Note: We do not collect or store any payment information.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for operational purposes:

  • To create, manage, and track your clinic appointments.
  • To send booking confirmations, status updates, and reminders via WhatsApp.
  • To help clinic staff efficiently manage their patient queue and availability.

We respect your privacy: We do not sell your personal data or communications to any third parties.

4. WhatsApp Messaging

Our platform relies on WhatsApp to facilitate bookings:

  • We use the Meta WhatsApp Business API to securely send and receive messages.
  • Messages from ReceptionAI are only sent in response to patient-initiated conversations.
  • Patients can opt out or stop receiving messages at any time simply by not initiating further conversations or by following any provided opt-out instructions.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • All data is stored securely on servers located within India, in compliance with local regulations.
  • We retain appointment and booking data for a period of 1 year to assist clinics with patient records and follow-ups.
  • We implement industry-standard security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information.

6. Your Rights

You have control over your personal data:

  • Right to Deletion: You can request the deletion of your data from our systems by contacting us.
  • Right to Access: You can ask us what specific data we hold about you at any time.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us:

  • Email: support.receptionai@gmail.com
  • Website: receptionai.in

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page or through appropriate communication channels.
