The Service

ReceptionAI provides clinic partners with:

  • An AI-powered WhatsApp bot that handles patient appointment booking conversations
  • An admin dashboard to manage doctors, schedules, appointments, and settings
  • Automated WhatsApp message templates for appointment confirmations and reminders
  • A knowledge base feature to answer patient queries about the clinic's services

ReceptionAI is a booking and communication tool only. It does not provide medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or any healthcare service. All clinical decisions remain solely with the clinic and its medical staff.
